You can tell an AI agent "don't spend more than $500." It will agree. It will try. It will fail, not because it's dumb, but because a prompt is a suggestion, not a constraint.

We solved this for apps in 2006 with OAuth: scoped tokens, proof, revocation. AgentAuth applies the same idea to agents, with signed credentials, spend limits enforced at the infrastructure level, and immutable logs a CFO can actually read.
