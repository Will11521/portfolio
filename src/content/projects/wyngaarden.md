## The problem

Wyngaarden Pastries & Artisan Bread, an independent bakery in Ottawa, needed a digital presence that matched the care that goes into its bread: one that customers could find, trust and order from.

## What I built

With my team, The Dough Crew, we redesigned the bakery's whole digital experience:

- **UX research.**
- **A visual identity** for the brand.
- **The website on Square Online,** with custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript where the platform needed it.
- **Product photography.**
- **A social media strategy.**

## Result

The site is live for a real client. We presented the project to a full room of faculty, industry professionals and peers at Algonquin College. Real client, real constraints, real deliverables.
